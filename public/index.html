1. Import a LinkedIn export

LinkedIn → Me → Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data → tick Connections → Request archive. LinkedIn emails you a ZIP with Connections.csv inside (step-by-step guide: docs/export-your-connections.md). Upload it here, or drop it into the data/ folder.
